Company Name: RS Bunda (Hospital Group)

Industry: Healthcare

Services: Google Ads Management, Search & Performance Max Campaigns, Keyword Strategy, Conversion Tracking, Segment-level Reporting

### Request

RS Bunda needed to fill its patient funnel across multiple specialist verticals — general lead generation, Laparoscopy, Ibu & Anak (Obstetrics), Ophthalmology, and Pediatrics. Healthcare advertising demands precision: budgets must reach people actively searching for care, and every segment's cost-per-lead has to be accountable.

### Action

* **Vertical keyword architecture:** Built keyword-targeted Search campaigns for each high-intent medical vertical, from general lead gen to specialist services like Laparoscopy and Obstetrics.
* **Performance Max deployment:** Ran PMax campaigns to capture demand across all Google surfaces beyond Search.
* **Segment-level measurement:** Tracked cost per conversion by segment so budget could flow to the most efficient verticals in real time.

### Result

In just two months (July 1 – August 31, 2026), the account delivered:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total account spend | IDR 152,898,515 |
| Total impressions | 1,703,412 |
| Total clicks | 69,600 (4.09% blended CTR) |
| Total conversions | 794.5 |
| Avg cost per conversion | IDR 192,441 |

**Cost per conversion by segment**

| Segment | Cost / conversion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brand terms | IDR 56,545 | 321.7 conversions — most efficient (3.4× cheaper than PMax) |
| Search (high-intent) | IDR 162,691 | 9.11% CTR on 264,802 impressions |
| Performance Max | IDR 276,939 | 206.9 conversions · 45,465 clicks |
| Specialist verticals | CV > 5% | Laparoscopy & Ibu & Anak high-intent prospects |

The segment breakdown gave the hospital a clear playbook: brand terms as the efficiency anchor, high-intent Search for scale, and PMax for incremental reach.
