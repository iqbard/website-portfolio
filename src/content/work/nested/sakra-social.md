Company Name: Sakra.id

Industry: Authentic Shoes Reselling / E-commerce

Services: Full Social Media Ownership, Content Strategy, Content Creation, Community Management

### Request

Sakra.id sells authentic sneakers to a youth market that lives on Instagram and TikTok — an audience that can smell inauthenticity instantly. The brand needed full social media ownership: not consultation, but someone running the channel end-to-end and being accountable for sales.

### Action

* **Channel strategy:** Built the content calendar around sneaker culture, drops, and authenticity proof — the trust currency of the resell market.
* **Daily content creation:** Produced platform-native content for the youth audience, from drop announcements to sneaker care and styling.
* **Community management:** Engaged directly with buyers and sneakerheads to build the credibility a reseller needs.

### Result

* **Profit +~40% in the first month** of full social ownership.

For a reselling business, where margins live and die on trust and timing, social became the primary sales driver — not just a storefront window.
