Company Name: Shift Academy

Industry: EdTech / Online Education

Services: SEO Audit, Spam Cleanup, Site Health Recovery, SERP Re-ranking

### Request

The website had been compromised. Spam keywords and 404 pages were creeping into Google's index, damaging both brand perception and search rankings. Shift Academy needed the damage contained and its rankings restored — without losing the equity the site had already built.

### Action

* **Diagnosis:** Audited the site to map the full extent of the hack — off-topic/spam keywords polluting the index and 404 pages eroding site health.
* **Spam cleanup:** Cleared all off-topic and spam keywords from the indexed content.
* **Site health rebuild:** Removed every 404 page and rebuilt the site's technical health signals.
* **Re-ranking:** Re-submitted and re-optimized content so Google could re-crawl and re-rank the cleaned site.

### Result

* **#1 on Google SERP** for related keywords — plus additional #5 first-page rankings.
* SEO stats after recovery: 200,000 impressions · 3,000 clicks · 1.4% avg CTR.

A crisis became a case study in technical SEO recovery: from hacked and de-ranked to holding the top result for the brand's core queries.
