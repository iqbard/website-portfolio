Company Name: Borong Indonesia

Industry: B2B Fintech / Wholesale Supply Chain (YC W20)

Services: Performance Marketing Strategy, User Acquisition, Paid Media Execution, Funnel Analytics, Reporting

### Request

Borong's products live or die on adoption by small retailers. Borong POS and Borong Direct each needed a steady, measurable stream of new users — acquired efficiently, attributed correctly, and scaled without waste. As an in-house specialist, the mandate covered the full acquisition funnel with no hand-offs.

### Action

* **Full-stack ownership:** Owned strategy, creative, execution, and reporting end-to-end — no agency relay, faster iteration loops.
* **Product-led acquisition:** Aligned campaigns with the distinct value propositions of Borong POS and Borong Direct, targeting retailers at the moment of operational need.
* **Omnichannel discipline:** Applied the same data-driven scaling playbook used across Shopee, Google Ads, and Meta — treating channels as one funnel rather than competing budgets.
* **Pre/post-campaign analysis:** Measured every push with CRO and ROAS maximization in mind, feeding learnings directly back into creative and targeting.

### Result

The role continues today, but the operating model is the result: a single owner for the entire paid acquisition stack at a YC-backed fintech, responsible for turning ad spend into activated retailers across two products. It is the in-house counterpart to the agency-side work in this portfolio — the same funnel logic, applied where the product and the budget live under one roof.
