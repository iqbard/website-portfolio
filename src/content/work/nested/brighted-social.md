Company Name: Brighted.id

Industry: EdTech / SBMPTN Video Learning Platform

Services: Social Media Content Creation, Content Strategy

### Request

Brighted.id was a Depok-based edutech startup competing for the attention of high school students preparing for SBMPTN — an audience that spends hours a day on social media and ignores anything that feels like an ad. The brand needed content that felt native to their feed.

### Action

* **Audience-native content:** Created social content in the formats and tone Indonesian students actually engage with — exam tips, study motivation, and product highlights.
* **Exam-calendar alignment:** Timed content around the SBMPTN prep cycle so every post landed when the audience needed it most.
* **Brand building for a startup:** Established a consistent visual identity across the young company's channels.

### Result

A consistent, native social presence that turned Brighted.id from an unknown startup into a recognizable name for students preparing for one of the most important exams of their lives — feeding top-of-funnel awareness for the video learning platform.
