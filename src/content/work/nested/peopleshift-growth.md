Company Name: Peopleshift (Shift Academy sister brand)

Industry: Employee Development Consulting

Services: Social Media Management, Content Research & Design, Meta Ads, WordPress Development, Site Security

### Request

Peopleshift serves a different audience from its sister brand — companies investing in employee development rather than individuals learning new skills. It needed its own content voice, its own lead engine, and its own website, built from zero.

### Action

* **Social media ownership:** Ran content research, briefs, and design, plus day-to-day account management for the brand's channels.
* **Meta Ads program:** Launched and managed paid campaigns tuned to B2B service lead generation.
* **WordPress build:** Developed and secured **peopleshift.id** on WordPress, including ongoing content and maintenance.

### Result

* **Social growth (Jan–Mar 2023, QoQ):** followers **+14.4%**, reach **+5,000%**.
* **Meta Ads (2023):** IDR 20M spend · 2.5% CTR · IDR 8,000 cost per lead · 10.3% CVR.
* **peopleshift.id:** live, secure, and maintained as the brand's digital home.

Two channels, one quarter: organic reach up fifty-fold while paid leads arrived at IDR 8,000 each with a double-digit conversion rate.
