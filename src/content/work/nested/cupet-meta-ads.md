Company Name: Cupet

Industry: Pet Dating Platform / Mobile App

Services: Meta Ads Management, Audience Testing, Conversion Rate Optimization

### Request

Cupet needed to validate paid acquisition for a novel product — a dating platform for pet owners. With a deliberately small test budget, the goal was not scale but proof: could the concept convert cold social traffic into signups at a viable cost?

### Action

* **Lean test design:** Structured the campaign to extract maximum learning from a IDR 500K budget.
* **Audience targeting:** Targeted pet-owner communities where the product's premise lands instantly.
* **Conversion focus:** Optimized for completed signups, not vanity engagement metrics.

### Result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total spend (2023) | IDR 500K |
| Avg CTR | 3.0% |
| Cost per lead | IDR 15,000 |
| Conversion rate | **26%** — best in portfolio |

One in four people who clicked signed up. The test validated Cupet's acquisition thesis and identified IDR 15,000 as the ceiling cost per user for scale.
