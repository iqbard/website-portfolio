Company Name: Titik Pulang

Industry: Lifestyle & Community

Services: UI/UX Design, WordPress Development

Website: https://titikpulang.id

### Request

Despite having a strong physical community presence, Titik Pulang struggled to translate their brand's core values—warmth, mindfulness, and a sense of belonging—into their digital platforms.

Their existing website was sluggish, visually cluttered, and hard to navigate on mobile devices. They needed a complete digital revamp to lower their high bounce rates, simplify content discovery, and provide an intuitive backend system so their internal team could easily publish events, articles, and community updates without technical friction.

### Action

We initiated a comprehensive redesign and development phase focused on creating a digital "safe space" for their audience. We brought this vision to life through:

* Empathetic UI/UX Design: Conducted user research to design a calming, minimalist interface utilizing soft white and neutral tones, ensuring the website felt like a natural extension of their physical space.
* Custom WordPress Development: Built a fully bespoke, lightweight WordPress theme tailored specifically to their content needs, moving away from heavy, bloated plugins.
* Mobile-First Optimization: Completely restructured the mobile navigation journey to ensure frictionless reading and event registration on the go.
* Backend Streamlining: Customized the WordPress dashboard with advanced custom fields (ACF) so the Titik Pulang team could seamlessly update content without writing a single line of code.

### Result

The newly launched website successfully captured the brand's essence while significantly upgrading performance. Within the first three months of launch, Titik Pulang saw a 45% increase in average session duration, indicating that users were finally taking the time to read and engage with the content.

Additionally, mobile bounce rates dropped by 30%, and the internal team reported a massive reduction in the time it takes to publish new events. The new digital home proved that functional development paired with mindful design can turn a simple website into an active community hub.
