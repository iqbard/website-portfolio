Company Name: YUKK Indonesia

Industry: Fintech / Payment Gateway

Services: SEO, SEM / Google Ads, Keyword Strategy, Content Writing, CMS Publishing

### Request

YUKK operates in the payments space, where search visibility equals trust. The company needed stronger organic rankings for fintech and payment-industry queries, plus a paid acquisition channel that could generate leads at a defensible cost.

### Action

* **Technical & on-page SEO:** Optimized site structure and pages for payment-industry search intent.
* **Paid search campaigns:** Ran Google Ads with tight keyword targeting around fintech buyer queries.
* **Research-led content:** Researched fintech and payment industry topics through online sources, interviews, and studies, then wrote marketing copy for products and services — structured drafts via CMS for publishing.

### Result

**SEO results (March 2024)**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total impressions | 597,000 |
| Total clicks | 27,000 |
| Avg CTR | 4.5% |

**Google Ads campaign (February 2024)**

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total spend | IDR 68M |
| Avg CTR | 4.12% |
| Cost per lead | IDR 6,240 |
| Conversion rate | 3.58% |

A 4.5% organic CTR on nearly 600K impressions put YUKK's content in front of buyers actively comparing payment providers — while paid search filled the pipeline at IDR 6,240 per lead.
