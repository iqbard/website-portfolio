Company Name: Glass Studios

Industry: Fashion & Eyewear E-commerce (UK)

Services: Shopee Ads Campaign Management, Audience Research, Ad Copywriting, Budget & Bid Control, Performance Reporting

### Request

Glass Studios needed to scale its presence on Shopee without losing efficiency. The account was spending moderately with a 4.5% baseline CTR, and the brand wanted aggressive top-of-funnel impression capture while still converting purchases at the bottom of the funnel — a balance most marketplace campaigns never strike.

### Action

* **Budget scaling strategy:** Grew the monthly budget from IDR 37M (October 2025) to a peak of IDR 85M (March 2026) — a +130% increase — while protecting return on ad spend at every step.
* **Funnel-split campaign structure:** Separated top-of-funnel impression campaigns from bottom-funnel purchase campaigns, so reach gains never cannibalized conversion efficiency.
* **Continuous creative testing:** Iterated ad copy and creative angles against audience research insights to lift engagement.
* **Tight bid & budget control:** Daily monitoring and bid adjustments to keep cost-per-click efficient as spend ramped up.

### Result

Across ten months (September 2025 – June 2026) the account managed IDR 501M in total spend while efficiency improved rather than degraded:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total managed spend (10 months) | IDR 501M |
| Monthly budget scaling | IDR 37M (Oct '25) → IDR 85M peak (Mar '26), +130% |
| Peak daily CTR | 18.4% (Apr '26), up from a 4.5% baseline |
| Daily link clicks at peak | 800–1,400+ (from ~40–100/day) |
| Search impression share | #1, held through peak scaling |

The four-fold increase in daily link clicks at a higher CTR meant the brand captured dramatically more high-intent traffic exactly when it mattered — during the peak scaling phase.
