Company Name: Shift Academy

Industry: EdTech / Online Education

Services: Social Media Management, Viral Reels Strategy, Content Creation, Scheduling, Community Management, Trend-jacking

### Request

Shift Academy's organic social was steady but invisible. The brand needed reach — the kind that makes an education company a household name among its target students — without paid budget doing the heavy lifting.

### Action

* **Viral Reels strategy:** Engineered short-form video concepts designed for shareability, riding platform trends while staying on-brand.
* **Daily content production:** Created and scheduled content daily, keeping the account perpetually active and algorithm-friendly.
* **Community management:** Managed comments and community interaction to convert viral attention into followers and profile visits.
* **Trend-jacking:** Moved fast on trending formats and sounds to capture reach windows as they opened.

### Result

* **Viral Reels:** 10.4M plays / 189K likes on a single video; 890K plays / 54K likes on the follow-up.
* **Growth (Jan–Mar 2023, QoQ):** followers **+44.3%**, reach **+6,600%**, profile visits **+21.9%**.

The account went from quiet to unmissable — with reach compounding seven quarters' worth of growth into one.
