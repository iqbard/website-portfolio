Company Name: Rolin Noris

Industry: Music / Indie Folk

Services: Artist Collaboration, Release Support, Promotion

### Request

Independent musicians wear every hat, and promotion is the hat that fits worst. Rolin Noris needed support for the release of his single **"Karikatur Ruam Ingatan"** — getting the track in front of listeners without a label machine behind it.

### Action

* **Artist collaboration:** Worked directly with the artist on the release plan for the single.
* **Release support:** Coordinated promotion around the launch window so the track's first impression counted.

### Result

Release support for **"Karikatur Ruam Ingatan"** — an independent single that reached its audience through collaboration rather than budget, exactly how indie folk is supposed to work.
