Company Name: Onlashes Beauty Clinic

Industry: Beauty / Aesthetic Clinic

Services: Meta Ads Management, Lead Generation, Conversion Tracking

### Request

Onlashes Beauty Clinic wanted to fill its appointment calendar through paid social. Beauty is a competitive Meta category, so the campaign needed creative that stopped the scroll and a funnel that converted clicks into booked leads.

### Action

* **Campaign setup:** Built Meta lead campaigns targeting high-intent beauty audiences.
* **Creative iteration:** Tested ad angles against engagement and lead-quality signals.
* **Cost discipline:** Monitored cost per lead weekly to protect acquisition economics.

### Result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total spend (Mar–Apr 2024) | IDR 32M |
| Avg CTR | 2.15% |
| Cost per lead | IDR 8,700 |
| Conversion rate | 13.5% |

A 13.5% conversion rate on IDR 8,700 per lead gave the clinic a reliable, repeatable pipeline of appointment leads.
