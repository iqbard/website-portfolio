Company Name: Glass Studios

Industry: Fashion & Eyewear E-commerce (UK)

Services: Meta Ads Management, Interest & Remarketing Targeting, Dynamic Catalog Ads, Omnichannel Funnel Strategy

### Request

Glass Studios already had a strong Shopee Ads program, but Meta was running in isolation. They wanted to know whether social demand generation could meaningfully lower acquisition costs — or whether it would just add another line to the budget.

### Action

* **Omnichannel funnel design:** Connected Meta Ads with the existing Shopee Ads program — top-of-funnel Meta interest targeting feeding high-intent marketplace capture. One audience, two capture points.
* **Custom audience building:** Built interest and remarketing audiences from Shopee behavior signals to warm up prospects before they hit the marketplace.
* **Dynamic catalog ads:** Deployed dynamic product catalogs so every prospect saw the exact eyewear they had already shown intent toward.
* **Traffic routing:** Routed social engagement traffic directly to checkout paths instead of dead-end brand pages.

### Result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Blended CAC | −28% (custom audiences + dynamic catalog ads) |
| Social engagement lift | 3.4×, with traffic routed to checkout |
| Channels in synergy | Meta (interest & remarketing) + Shopee (catalog capture) |

The omnichannel loop proved the thesis: social demand generation did not just add reach — it made every marketplace rupiah work harder, cutting blended acquisition cost by more than a quarter.
