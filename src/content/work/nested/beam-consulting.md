Company Name: Beam Consulting

Industry: Behavioral Consulting (UK)

Services: Website Development from Client Designs, Server Maintenance, Email Maintenance, Lead Generation Support

### Request

Beam Consulting had its designs ready but needed a partner to turn them into a working website — and, just as importantly, to keep the server and email infrastructure that captures consulting leads running without incident.

### Action

* **Pixel-faithful build:** Developed the website from the client-provided designs, preserving their brand system exactly.
* **Infrastructure ownership:** Maintained the server and email setup that powers the firm's lead generation.
* **Reliability first:** For a consulting firm, a down contact form is a lost engagement — uptime and deliverability were treated as features.

### Result

A professional web presence that matches the firm's designs, backed by server and email infrastructure that simply works — leaving Beam Consulting free to focus on clients while inbound leads keep arriving.
