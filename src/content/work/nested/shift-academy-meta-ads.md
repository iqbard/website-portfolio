Company Name: Shift Academy

Industry: EdTech / Online Education

Services: Meta Ads Management, Creative Strategy, Lead Generation, Conversion Tracking

### Request

Shift Academy needed to fill its course funnel with qualified leads at a cost that made enrollment economics work. The benchmark campaign in June 2022 had to prove whether paid social could deliver at scale for an education brand in Indonesia.

### Action

* **Campaign architecture:** Structured Meta campaigns around course intent and audience research, separating prospecting from retargeting.
* **Creative testing:** Iterated ad creative and copy against audience response to protect lead quality as spend increased.
* **Full-funnel measurement:** Tracked cost per lead and conversion rate end-to-end, not just clicks.

### Result

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Total spend | IDR 150M |
| Avg CTR | 3.0% |
| Cost per lead | IDR 6,000 |
| Conversion rate | 15.5% |

A 15.5% conversion rate at IDR 6,000 per lead made paid social the academy's most dependable acquisition channel — the benchmark every later campaign was measured against.
